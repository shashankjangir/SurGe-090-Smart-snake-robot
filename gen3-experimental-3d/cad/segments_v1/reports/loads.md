## Load cases

**Grounded undulation**, upper bound: one yaw joint swings the whole tail-side chain with the others locked, μ = 0.5 (**ASSUMED**).

| Joint | N·m |
|---|---|
| J2 | 1.395 |
| J4 | 0.857 |
| J6 | 0.450 |
| J8 | 0.174 |
| J10 | 0.029 |

J2/J4 exceed both allowables in this bound. Real gaits share the motion between joints, so measure joint current
before raising amplitude.

**Cantilever (supported bend, lifted span), straight and horizontal:**

| Pitch joint | Head side up (N·m) | Tail side up (N·m) |
|---|---|---|
| J1 | 0.014 | 3.425 |
| J3 | 0.227 | 2.219 |
| J5 | 0.703 | 1.274 |
| J7 | 1.440 | 0.591 |
| J9 | 2.439 | 0.170 |

Largest lifted span within the allowable (both variants): **head plus two links from J3**, or **tail plus one link from J9**.
This is unchanged from revision 1, though the margins are smaller (J3 at 0.227 against 0.245 for the 7.4 V variant). The intended span is **owner input**.

**Tail:** 155.8 g. Lifting it from J9 needs 0.170 N·m, which is within both allowables.

**Cable drag:** negligible change in length now, since the lead crosses on the joint axis. Only the bending stiffness of the lead
acts, which is unmeasured.

**Handling:** lifting the robot by one end loads J1 or J9 to about 2.4–3.4 N·m, above the 7.4 V stall torque (1.9 N·m).
**Support it under at least every third link, power off.**

## Strength

There is no strength certification. Layer-oriented PETG coupons are needed first. The weak layer direction is local Y for MID and TAIL
(printed with +Y on the bed). The loads across it are the face-A screw pull-up and the standoff pull-out.
